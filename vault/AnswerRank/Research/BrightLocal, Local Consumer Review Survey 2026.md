# BrightLocal, Local Consumer Review Survey 2026

[Read the source](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)

## What it found
45% of consumers used AI tools such as ChatGPT to find local businesses in the past year (6% the year before). 74% prioritise reviews from the last three months; 32% want one from the last two weeks. 68% require at least 4 stars; 31% only use businesses rated 4.5 or higher.

## So AnswerRank
Review work aims at a new review at least every two weeks, not a count. Retention watches for a client going quiet on reviews. The 45% figure is what the sales script leans on.
