# Gong Labs, cold calling statistics

[Read the source](https://www.gong.io/blog/cold-call-stats)

## What it found
Successful cold calls average 5m50s, about twice as long as unsuccessful ones; reps talk about 55% of the time, in longer stretches (53s vs 25s). Wednesday and Thursday are the best days.

## So AnswerRank
The script's middle is a 40-second explanation, not a string of questions. The call list marks Wednesday and Thursday.
