# FTC, CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business

[Read the source](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)

## What it found
Any opt-out mechanism must process requests for at least 30 days after the message is sent, and opt-outs must be honoured within 10 business days. The postal address may be a street address, a USPS PO Box or a private mailbox at a registered commercial mail receiving agency.

## So AnswerRank
No email is sent, by the fleet or from the phone, unless the unsubscribe link answers from the internet, and the server that answers it runs all the time.
