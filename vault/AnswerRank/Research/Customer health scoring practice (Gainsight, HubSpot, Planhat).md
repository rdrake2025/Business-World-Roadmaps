# Customer health scoring practice (Gainsight, HubSpot, Planhat)

[Read the source](https://www.gainsight.com/blog/customer-health-scores/)

## What it found
The red band should be small enough to act on; scores built on weak signals give false confidence and false alarms. Thresholds should be tested against outcomes and adjusted.

## So AnswerRank
'Act now' needs a concrete risk: a failing card, no report delivered, fixes still not live after three weeks, a falling score, or long silence with nothing moving. A quiet client whose report arrives on time is monitored, not flagged.
