# Help-desk practice on saved replies (Zendesk, Intercom, HelpDesk)

[Read the source](https://www.helpdesk.com/blog/canned-responses/)

## What it found
Saved replies suit simple, repetitive, informational questions and should not be used for sensitive cases such as cancellations or bad news. They need clear names, an owner, and regular review to stay accurate.

## So AnswerRank
Only answers you wrote yourself are saved for reuse, each under the words that trigger it, listed in one place to review or delete. Cancellations and refunds are never answered from a saved reply.
