# Google, Send attachments with your Gmail message

[Read the source](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/6584)

## What it found
Gmail and standard Workspace plans send attachments up to 25 MB and receive up to 50 MB; attachments are base64-encoded, which adds about a third, so the largest file that fits is under about 18 MB.

## So AnswerRank
The weekly off-server backup is emailed to you when it's under 15 MB compressed; above that, the email says so and how to fetch it.
