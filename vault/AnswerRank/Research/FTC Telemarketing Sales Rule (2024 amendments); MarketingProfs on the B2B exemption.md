# FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule (2024 amendments); MarketingProfs on the B2B exemption

[Read the source](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/03/ftc-implements-new-protections-businesses-against-telemarketing-fraud-affirms-protections-against-ai)

## What it found
Most business-to-business calls are exempt from the Do Not Call registry and much of the Rule. Since 2024, misrepresentations are prohibited on business calls too. The exemption does not cover turning a business's 'no need' into a pitch to the person who answered (MarketingProfs).

## So AnswerRank
Calls are dialled by hand to published business numbers; the script quotes only real audit results and never claims to be from an AI company; 'not interested' ends calls and email at once.
