# Whitespark, 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors (local pack)

[Read the source](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)

## What it found
Top local pack factors: primary Google Business Profile category, proximity to the searcher, keywords in the GBP business title, a physical address in the city searched, open at the time of search. Review recency ranks #11. GBP signals weigh 32% overall, reviews 20%.

## So AnswerRank
Getting the primary category exactly right is the first GBP task. Hours are kept accurate because being open when someone searches counts.
