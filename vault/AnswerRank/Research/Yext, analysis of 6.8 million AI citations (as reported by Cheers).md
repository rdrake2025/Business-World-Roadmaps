# Yext, analysis of 6.8 million AI citations (as reported by Cheers)

[Read the source](https://www.cheers.tech/geo-academy/ai-search-engine-source-differences)

## What it found
86% of AI citations came from sources a business manages: its own website (44%) and its listings (42%).

## So AnswerRank
Most of what the engines quote is within a client's control, which is what the retainer sells: the website and the listings, kept right.
