# Google Workspace, Pricing

[Read the source](https://workspace.google.com/pricing)

## What it found
Business Starter is $8.40 per user a month on the flexible plan and $7 on annual billing. App passwords still work for IMAP and SMTP with 2-Step Verification; plain passwords stopped working for Workspace on 14 March 2025.

## So AnswerRank
The mailbox is the one fixed cost that can't be avoided: the fleet sends over SMTP and reads replies over IMAP with an app password.
