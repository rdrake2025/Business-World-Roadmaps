# Instantly, Cold Email Benchmark Report 2026

[Read the source](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)

## What it found
Average reply rate 3.43%; top quartile 5.5%+; elite 10.7%+. 58% of replies come from the first email. 4-7 touches is the sweet spot. Elite first emails average under 80 words. Wednesday has the highest reply rates. Keep bounces under 2%.

## So AnswerRank
The first email carries most of the weight and is kept under 80 words; the checker rejects a first touch over 100. The sequence is four touches. Bounces over 2% pause cold sending. The Analyst compares our reply rate against 3.4% and 5.5%.
