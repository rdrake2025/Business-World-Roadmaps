# Gong Labs, cold call opening lines (300 million calls)

[Read the source](https://www.gong.io/blog/cold-call-opening-lines)

## What it found
Stating the reason for the call raises success 2.1x. 'Did I catch you at a bad time?' makes a meeting 40% less likely.

## So AnswerRank
The call script opens with who is calling and the reason, in one breath, and never asks if it's a bad time.
