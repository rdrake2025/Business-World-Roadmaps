# Microsoft, Outlook requirements for high-volume senders

[Read the source](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftdefenderforoffice365blog/strengthening-email-ecosystem-outlook%E2%80%99s-new-requirements-for-high%E2%80%90volume-senders/4399730)

## What it found
From 5 May 2025, domains sending 5,000+ emails a day to Outlook.com, Hotmail and Live must pass SPF and DKIM and publish DMARC (p=none at least, aligned), or be filtered or rejected.

## So AnswerRank
Same bar as Gmail, applied from the first email.
