# Agents

18 agents run the business, in this order each cycle. Each one has a
researcher that checks its work and asks one question.

| Agent | Runs | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Concierge]] | every 30 minutes | Handles inbound replies and drafts the response. |
| [[Guardian]] | every 30 minutes | Watches Autopilot and pauses any part that starts going wrong. |
| [[Onboarder]] | every 30 minutes | Writes the welcome the hour a client signs, not the week after. |
| [[Scout]] | every 6 hours | Discovers local service businesses and adds them to the prospect pool. |
| [[Prospector]] | every 2 hours | Finds the published contact address for discovered businesses. |
| [[Auditor]] | every hour | Runs teaser audits on prospects and full audits for clients. |
| [[Citations]] | once a day | Checks the sites AI engines quote, for clients and hot prospects. |
| [[Fixer]] | every 6 hours | Generates schema, FAQ copy, GBP and citation deliverables from audits. |
| [[Reporter]] | every 12 hours | Renders and files monthly client visibility reports. |
| [[Outreach]] | every 4 hours | Drafts compliant, evidence-backed outreach for audited prospects. |
| [[Sender]] | every 15 minutes | Sends what you approved, at the right hours, so you never tap Send. |
| [[Bookkeeper]] | every 2 hours | Confirms payments, chases unpaid clients, bills paying ones, and reports progress to target. |
| [[Retention]] | once a day | Scores client health and names the one action that saves each account. |
| [[Explorer]] | every 12 hours | Samples candidate markets to find the next vertical worth entering. |
| [[Analyst]] | every 12 hours | Reads outcomes and reports what is actually converting. |
| [[Strategist]] | once a day | Reads market evidence and recommends the single next move. |
| [[Briefing]] | every 15 minutes | Emails you the day's list each morning, and alerts when it matters. |
| [[Researcher]] | every 6 hours | Runs a researcher against every agent and reports what holds. |
