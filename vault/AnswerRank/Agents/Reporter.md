# Reporter

**Renders and files monthly client visibility reports.** Runs every 12 hours.

## How it works
The report is the retention mechanism. A client renews when they can see the number moving and know what to do next, so every report carries three things: the score with its trend, the competitor gap that made them buy, and a prioritised action list tied to the specific questions they lost.

## Its researcher asks
Do the monthly reports start a conversation?
