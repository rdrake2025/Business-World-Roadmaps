# Dashboard

Your live numbers appear here once AnswerRank writes to your own vault: in Keys and settings, give it your vault's folder. It's refreshed every hour while AnswerRank runs: what needs you today (the same list as the Today tab), the money coming in, and the dates coming up for each pilot: the next measurement, the before-and-after, the offer and the end of the free months.
