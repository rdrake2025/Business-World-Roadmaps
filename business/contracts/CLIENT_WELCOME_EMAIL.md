# Client Onboarding Sequence

Three emails. Send the first within an hour of payment — the gap between paying
and hearing from you is where buyer's remorse lives.

---

## Email 1 — immediately after payment

**Subject:** You're in — here's what happens next

```
Hi [NAME],

Thanks for signing up. Here's exactly what happens now, so there are no
surprises.

TODAY
I'm running your full audit — 10 buyer-intent questions across ChatGPT,
Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Claude.

WITHIN 24 HOURS
You get your first report. It shows your visibility score, every question
I tested with the actual AI answer, who's being named instead of you, and
a prioritised action list.

WITH THE REPORT
- LocalBusiness + FAQPage structured data, ready to paste
- Answer-shaped content written against the questions you're losing
- Your Google Business Profile action list
- A directory coverage checklist

WHAT I NEED FROM YOU
Just one thing: a reply confirming your exact business name, address and
phone as they appear on your Google Business Profile. Those three have to
match byte-for-byte everywhere, and it's the most common thing that's
quietly wrong.

WHAT TO EXPECT, HONESTLY
Month 1 is the baseline. Month 2 is where you'll see movement, if the
deliverables get installed. I'll be straight with you either way — if
something isn't working, I'd rather tell you than let you find out.

Any questions, just reply. I read everything.

[YOUR NAME]
[PHONE]
```

## Email 2 — with the first report

**Subject:** Your AI visibility report — [BUSINESS NAME]

```
Hi [NAME],

Your first report is attached.

The headline: you're currently named in [X] of 10 AI answers for
[vertical] in [city]. [COMPETITOR] is named in [Y].

Start with these three, in this order:

1. [TOP ACTION FROM REPORT]
2. [SECOND ACTION]
3. [THIRD ACTION]

Number one is the schema — it's a copy-paste job into your site's <head>
and it's the fastest single move on the score. If your web person handles
the site, forward them the two .json files and this line:

  "Please add both of these as JSON-LD script tags in the site <head>."

That's it. Five minutes of their time.

Want me to walk you through the report? 15 minutes, any time this week —
[BOOKING LINK].

[YOUR NAME]
```

## Email 3 — day 14

**Subject:** Quick check-in — anything installed yet?

```
Hi [NAME],

Two weeks in. Not chasing — just checking whether the schema and FAQ
content made it onto the site.

I ask because it's the difference between your month-2 report showing
movement and showing the same number again. The businesses that see their
score climb are, almost without exception, the ones who installed things
in the first three weeks.

If it's stuck on your end, tell me where and I'll help. If you'd rather I
just did it for you, the Managed plan covers implementation and I can
switch you over.

[YOUR NAME]
```

---

## Operator notes

**The day-14 email is your retention lever.** A client who installs nothing
sees a flat score in month two and cancels in month three, and they will blame
you rather than themselves. Catching that at day 14 is the difference between
90% retention and 60%.

**Every quiet client is a churning client.** Silence is not satisfaction. Call
anyone who has not replied by day 21.

**Upgrade path.** A client who cannot get things installed is not a problem
client — they are a Managed client who has not been asked yet. That is a
$1,000/month upgrade, and it solves their actual problem.
