## 1. What we do

We measure how often your business is named in answers produced by AI
assistants and search engines — including ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews,
Perplexity and Claude — and we produce materials intended to improve that.

Each month, on the `[PLAN NAME]` plan, you receive:

- A visibility audit across `[10 / 20]` buyer-intent questions in your market
- A written report with your visibility score, its trend, and the competitors
  named alongside or instead of you
- `LocalBusiness` and `FAQPage` structured data, ready to install
- Answer-shaped content written against the questions you are currently losing
- A prioritised action list covering your Google Business Profile and directory
  listings

`[For Managed plan: We implement the above on your behalf where you grant access.]`

## 2. What we do not do

**We do not guarantee that your business will appear in any AI answer, at any
position, at any time.**

AI engines are operated by third parties. They change their models without
notice, and they produce different answers to the same question at different
times. No provider — at any price — can guarantee placement in them. Anyone who
tells you otherwise is not being straight with you.

What we guarantee is the work: the audit runs, the report is delivered, and the
deliverables are produced every cycle, on time.

## 3. What you agree to do

Results depend materially on implementation. You agree to:

- Install the deliverables we provide, or grant us access to install them
- Provide accurate information about your business
- Hold the rights to any material you ask us to publish on your behalf
- Respond to access requests within a reasonable time

**Deliverables left unused will not move your score.** This is the single most
common reason an engagement underperforms.

## 5. First-month guarantee

If, after 30 days, your visibility score has not increased **and** you have
installed the deliverables we provided, we will refund your first month in full.

This applies to the first month only. Installation is a condition of it — we
will ask you to confirm what was installed.

## 6. Term and cancellation

This Agreement runs month to month. Either party may cancel with **30 days'
written notice** by email. Service continues to the end of the paid period, and
you keep every deliverable already provided.

We may terminate immediately for non-payment, or if asked to do anything
unlawful or deceptive.

## 7. Ownership

Deliverables produced specifically for you — structured data, content, action
lists, reports — become **your property** once paid for.

Our measurement methodology, scoring system, prompt sets and software remain
**our property**. Nothing here transfers a licence to them.

## 8. Confidentiality

Neither party will disclose the other's non-public business information. We may
describe the engagement in general terms (industry, market, results achieved)
in marketing only with your **written permission**.

## 9. Data and access

Where you grant us access to your website, Google Business Profile, or other
systems, we will:

- Use that access only to perform the services described here
- Hold credentials in a password manager, never in plain text
- Return or destroy credentials within 14 days of termination

claim**.

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses, including lost
profits, lost business, or loss of data.

Nothing here limits liability for fraud, gross negligence, or anything that
cannot lawfully be limited.

### Operator notes — delete before sending

- **Use e-signature.** Free tiers exist and a signed PDF beats a paper scan.
- **Do not negotiate clause 2.** The no-guarantee clause is what protects you
  from the one lawsuit that would end this business. A client who insists on a
  ranking guarantee is a client you decline.
- **Charge on signature**, never in arrears. SMB retainers invoiced after the
  fact get paid late or not at all.
- **Keep the term month-to-month.** Low friction to buy, and it removes any
  consumer-protection argument about lock-in. Your retention comes from the
  score moving, not from a contract.
